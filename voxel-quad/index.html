Privacy Policy (English)

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Voxel Quad (localized in Russian as «Квадро Трофи», Android package name com.ximvtech.voxelquad), developed and published by ximvtech.

1. Zero Data Collection & Zero Sharing

Voxel Quad does not collect, store, transmit, process, sell, or share any personal data, sensitive user data, or device information. No user registration, login, or account creation is required or offered.

2. 100% Offline Operation & Zero Device Permissions

  • The game operates 100% offline directly on your device.
  • The Android application manifest requests zero Android device permissions — it does not even request the android.permission.INTERNET permission.
  • Because the app has no network permission, it is technically incapable of transmitting any information off your device.

3. No Ads, No Analytics, and No Third-Party SDKs

  • No Advertisements: The app contains zero advertising banners, interstitials, or ad networks.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The app includes zero analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting, fingerprinting, or tracking libraries.
  • No Third-Party Data SDKs & No In-App Purchases: No external services receive any data from your device.

4. Local Game Progress Only

Game preferences (such as sound mute state and completed campaign levels) are stored strictly locally on your own device within the app's private sandbox storage. You can erase this local state at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the application.

5. Children’s Privacy & Family Safety

Voxel Quad is safe for players of all ages, including children. Because the app operates completely offline, contains no ads or external links during gameplay, and collects zero personal or device information from anyone, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR, and Google Play's Families Policy requirements.

6. Changes to This Policy & Contact

If future versions of Voxel Quad ever change how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated prior to release. If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or apps published by ximvtech, please contact the developer via GitHub Issues at: https://github.com/maximv/ximvtech-privacy/issues.
